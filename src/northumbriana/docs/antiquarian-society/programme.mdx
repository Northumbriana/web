## Lectures

:::tip[Information]

Lecture meetings are held at 7.15 pm (doors open for teas/coffees at 7.00 pm) on the last Friday of most months September to June in St James’ Centre, Wellway, Morpeth, NE61 1BN

**Donations requested at door: £1 suggested for members or £4 for visitors**

**Annual subscription: £25**

Visitors and new members always welcome!

This programme can also be [downloaded as a PDF](pdfs/programme-2026-27.pdf)

:::
